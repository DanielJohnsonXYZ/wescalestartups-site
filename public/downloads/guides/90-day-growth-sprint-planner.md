## How to use

## 3. Weekly rhythm
- Monday: read the dashboard (one source of truth)
- Wednesday: ship tests / creative / outreach
- Friday: decide — scale, kill, or iterate

## 4. Transfer checklist (week 12–13)
- [ ] Dashboard owned by someone on the team
- [ ] Decision cadence documented and kept
- [ ] Experiment cards and learnings archived
- [ ] Founder not in every growth loop
