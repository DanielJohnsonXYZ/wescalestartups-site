**Tooling note, not a site defect: programmatic scroll does not move this site.** `window.scrollTo` and `Element.scrollIntoView` both leave `window.scrollY` at 0 — a smooth-scroll library intercepts them. Two apparent "the embed failed to load" readings this run were this, not a fault. **Scroll with `computer`, never with injected JS, when testing lazy-loaded elements on this site.**

## The one new friction candidate — `/book`, mobile, and it is no-touch

Resolved after the bridge came back, so this is measured rather than suspected.

On a 375px viewport, once the embed loads:

- `#book-cal-inline` computes to **`overflow-y: auto`**, `clientHeight` **698px**, `scrollHeight` **956px**
- the Cal.com iframe is **956px tall**

**The calendar is not clipped. It sits in a nested scroll box.** A mobile visitor has to scroll *inside* a 698px window to reach the bottom 258px of the calendar — which on Cal.com's `month_view` is where the time slots and the confirm step live. Nested vertical scrolling inside a vertically-scrolling page is a well-known mobile friction pattern: the inner box captures the gesture, or the visitor never registers that there is more below the fold of the widget.

**Label this honestly: a friction *candidate* found by inspection, not a demonstrated constraint.** With Clarity dark there is no behavioural evidence that mobile visitors actually stall there, and no device split to say how many visitors are on mobile at all. It is also the single highest-value question to put to Clarity next run.

**It is no-touch regardless.** FIXED RULE 1 covers the embed's container explicitly. This is a report to Daniel, not a fix, and not something to route around.
