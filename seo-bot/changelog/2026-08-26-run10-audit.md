## The finding

**`/insights/venture-capital-marketing` is unknown to Google.** GSC URL inspection returns verdict NEUTRAL, `coverageState: "URL is unknown to Google"`, **no crawl record at all**. The page is 200, in the sitemap, and was submitted to Bing yesterday. It is the 4,161-word restoration of the best-performing content this site has ever had (the legacy URLs held 8,055 impressions at positions 7.4 and 20.0).

Cause, found in the `dist` link graph: **it had two internal inbound links** — its own page and the `/insights` hub. Nothing else on the site pointed at it. A sitemap entry alone is a weak discovery signal for a brand-new URL on a low-authority domain.

Fixed in `d600c0f` + `a5f610c` + `2e54725`. `industryDetailRelatedReading` was one shared list rendered identically on all ten industry pages, so there was no way to link a market page to its own long-form guide. Added an optional `relatedReading` field to the industries schema, made the template prefer it, and gave `/industries/vc-support` a list led by the guide. That page is the right parent: its H1 is "Venture capital & portfolio marketing" and `f814831` already redirects both legacy VC URLs to it.

## Gap found, not fixed

`refresh-lastmod.mjs` resolves a route to its page file and content entry but **does not follow imports into components**. So the five `/resources/*` tool routes did not move despite `GrowthToolPage.astro` changing, and the shortened titles carry a stale `2026-08-20` lastmod that will not signal recrawl. Not hand-edited — the file is generated and the fix belongs in the script.

## Left for Daniel — bot cannot or should not do these

1. **Homepage hero dead clicks.** Users click the hero headline and sub-headline expecting links. Both carry emphasised inline spans (a pulsing `v9-live-dot`, `<em>`, `<strong>`) that read as interactive. A visual design judgement on Daniel's hero; the standing rule is not to ship CSS the bot cannot see.
2. **`/pricing` intermittent handler.** A dead click followed by a live click on the same element one second later suggests a slow-binding JS handler. Needs a real browser to diagnose, not a static audit.
3. **Off-site authority** — backlog 3. Wikidata, profile consistency. Still the thing that moves the head terms, still requires Daniel.
4. **The GTM guide** — backlog 1, needs his reconstruction.
5. **Four orphan pages**, indexable and in the sitemap with zero internal inbound links: `/seo-content-strategy`, `/when-growth-plateaus`, `/wss-calendar`, `/wss-scale-score`. Either link them or noindex them — that is a positioning decision about whether they should exist, not a hygiene fix.
