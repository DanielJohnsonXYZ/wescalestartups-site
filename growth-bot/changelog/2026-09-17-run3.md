### `/book/thanks` is the second ledger, not a false positive

Run 1 recorded `/book/thanks` as *"landed on directly — not a booking proxy."* The referrer data corrects that. Of 16 mobile sessions touching `/book*`, **9 came from `cal.wescalestartups.com`** — the post-booking redirect. People who book on the direct Cal.com link never touch the site's `/book` page but do land on `/book/thanks` afterwards.

So: `/book/thanks` **pageviews** over-count bookings (28 views against 10 bookings — reloads, revisits, reschedule confirmations). Its **distinct `email=` parameter values do not.** That list is an independent per-booking ledger, and it is the only instrument that caught Scott McKim at all.

**New standing rule: cross-check the Clarity `/book/thanks` URL list against Gmail every run. Reaching `/book/thanks` is NOT evidence of a website-sourced booking** — most of those arrivals come from the Cal.com domain, not from the site's booking page.
