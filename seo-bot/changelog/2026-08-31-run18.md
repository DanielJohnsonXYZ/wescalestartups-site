## 2. The most influential third-party page about WSS is wrong in five ways

`growth-division.com/blog/best-startup-growth-agencies-in-the-uk`, published **7 May 2026 — the day after the WordPress-to-Astro rebuild**, is the page both engines read. Checked in full this run against `src/site.ts`.

| It says | The site says |
| --- | --- |
| "Three tiers: Growth Strategy, Marketing Services and Consulting, Fractional CMO" | **Four services**: Growth Diagnosis, 90-Day Growth Sprint, Acquisition System Build, Fractional CMO |
| "Pricing: **Not publicly disclosed**" | £2k–£4k · £8k–£12k · £15k–£25k · £5k–£8k/mo, published |
| "Best stage: **Pre-seed to Series A**" | Post-PMF, Seed to Series B |
| "Primary channels: **Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO** … narrower channel breadth, primarily paid and SEO" | Diagnosis-first consultancy; the site sells against the paid-execution model |
| "London-based **agency**" | Not an agency, and the positioning is built against that word |

Two further problems. It attributes to WSS a **"+768% average client growth rate for 2024"** and **"£20M in revenue generated"** — neither figure appears anywhere in the repo, so a third party is publishing performance claims in WSS's name that WSS does not make. And it lists **"University of Cambridge"** as a client; the real relationship is Cambridge Judge, accelerator-side.

**This is where run 17's Google defect came from.** Run 17 logged Google filing WSS under "Paid Ads & Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO) Sprints" and called it positioning drift with no identified cause. The cause is a page that says WSS's primary channels are Meta Ads, Google Ads and SEO. Two independent engine defects, one source.

**The site cannot fix this.** `/facts/we-scale-startups` already states all of it correctly, and Google cites that page — its AI impressions went 7 → 17 this window, the biggest proportional mover on the report. The site is doing its part. The error lives on someone else's domain and propagates from there.

Queued in `OUTREACH.md` as **C-1**, drafted, unsent. It is a factual-correction request about an existing entry, not a request for inclusion — a different thing from the prohibition on asking competitors for listicle placement, but still Daniel's call to make and not the bot's.

## 3. `scale startup` — 860 impressions, and none of it is demand

The largest non-brand query on the site, and new: **1,040 impressions over 3 months, 860 of them in the last 28 days**, position 10.3, homepage, **zero clicks**.

Page 1, huge, accelerating, zero clicks — it looks like the best unclaimed opportunity on the site. It is not. The live SERP for `scale startup` is:

> scale.com · Wikipedia "Scale AI" · **wescalestartups.com** · LinkedIn "Scale AI" · Y Combinator "Scale AI" · Index Ventures "Scale" · Stripe "How to Scale a Startup"

Google reads `scale startup` as navigational intent for **Scale AI, the company**. WSS ranks third organically in front of an audience looking for something else entirely. This is the brand-name-collision class the playbook already warns about (`we scale ab`, `calconnect login`), now quantified precisely and much larger than previously understood.

**Consequences for how the numbers are read:**

- Impressions rose 5.46k → 6.15k this window (+690). `scale startup` alone grew by roughly that amount. **The impression growth is the Scale AI collision, not demand.**
- Site CTR reads 1.3% → 1.1%, which looks like a decline. Discount the 860: 67 clicks / 5,290 = **1.27%**. Flat, not down.
- **Checking the SERP took one fetch and turned the run's most attractive lead into a prohibition.** This is the fixed rule working exactly as written.

Added to the do-not-do list.

## Why nothing shipped

No candidate passes the gate.

- **`scale startup`**, the only large page-1 non-brand pool, is unwinnable and not demand (§3).
- **The sprint cluster** is inside run 15's verdict window until 2026-09-17.
- **`/insights/when-to-hire-fractional-cmo`** carries the strongest question-shaped query on the site — `should a series a startup hire a fractional cmo a growth agency or wait to hire a full-time vp of growth`, 40 impressions at **7.6**, zero clicks — and is inside run 5's window until 2026-09-08.
- **The fractional-CMO-vs-agency cluster** (`fractional cmo vs agency` 37 @ 33.1, `growth agency vs fractional cmo` 36 @ 23.4, `fractional cmo vs marketing agency` 42 @ 24.4) is authority-bound with two failed verdicts. Run 1's page needs no third rewrite.
- **`growth leader for post pmf startup`**, 36 impressions at 9.2, is provider-shaped demand already served by provider pages. No defect to fix.
- The **backlog-10 sweep** ran again and returned nothing new: no `/services/*`, `/pricing` or `/book` URL is the landing page for a `what is` / `how to` / `why` query outside the sprint cluster, which is already handled.

The constraint the evidence names today is off-site, and it is unusually specific: the one third-party page both engines read describes a different company at the wrong stage in the wrong category with the wrong prices. No page this bot can write changes that.
