### Finding 1 — it is not fanout, which the backlog assumed without checking

Run 24 left this open: *"16 impressions have not been checked for fanout shape."* **Checked. `how to scale b2b saas acquisition` is six words, has no punctuated/unpunctuated twin, and is not a full-sentence question.** It passes run 5's filter. Backlog 10's queued lead — `how does a post-pmf plg saas company scale acquisition without breaking unit economics` — does not, and the two should stop being treated as the same class.

**Status: `INFORMATIONAL-ON-COMMERCIAL`, the backlog-10 defect class, genuine.** A how-to query landing on a £15k–£25k service page.

### Finding 2 — and it is the larger one: 71% of this page's named impressions are a collision

**`scalable land acquisition teams` alone is 35 of 49 named impressions, at position 91.** Land acquisition is property development. It has nothing to do with what this page sells.

The playbook records this page as **"94 impr at 41.4, zero clicks"**. That average position is mostly a position-91 real-estate query dragging the mean. **The page's real topical demand is 13 impressions in 28 days at position 27.**

**This is the `/resources` lesson repeating exactly** — a page-level figure that looked interpretable and was composed of somebody else's query. The rule was recorded in run 23 (*check the query mix before believing any page-level figure*) and had not been applied here. **The entity-collision problem is not confined to `wescale.com` and `scale startup`: ordinary English service nouns collide too, and `acquisition` is a bad one.**

### Consequence for 2026-09-17

**Judge backlog 15 on 13 impressions at position 27, not 94 at 41.4.** Thirteen over 28 days is ~42 over 90 — above run 5's three, below anything that has ever justified a page on this site, and position 27 sits in the band where run 1 proved content alone does not move it.

**Not declined here; the window is not open.** But the entry as written overstates the case, and run 27 should read this before acting.

## Sweep rule earned this run
