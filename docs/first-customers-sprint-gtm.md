# First Customers Sprint — GTM Plan

> Aligned to the Operating Brief. Supersedes the archived Notion GTM plan. Do not revive membership, Crux bundling, 5-week / First-10 framing, or growthforai.com unless you consciously choose a public URL.

## Objective

Two parallel money tests:

1. **Institutional:** sell 1+ paid GTM Evidence Workshop (£2,500–5,000)
2. **Public:** fill founding sprint (4–8 seats at £750–1,000) via waitlist → £100 deposits

**North-star:** % of participating founders with a money-level signal or evidence-backed stop/pivot within 30 days of the sprint.

| Signal | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Green | 1 paid workshop **or** 8–10 deposits → proceed |
| Red | Under 4 deposits after a serious 4-week push **and** no workshop pipeline after 10 institutional conversations → no cohort; fold into WSS |

## Funnel (public founding cohort)

```
LinkedIn + communities + newsletter + CRM outreach
  → Waitlist (DM SPRINT / email)
  → £100 deposit
  → Diagnostic (accepted founders)
  → Enrolled 4–8
```

At 4–8 seats, direct conversations beat funnel theatre. Do not invent a teardown webinar or 300-person waitlist as a prerequisite.

**Directional ratios:** 40–60 serious waitlist conversations → 12–20 deposits offered → 8–10 deposits taken → 4–8 enrolled after diagnostic.

## Channel plan

### A. Institutional (cash + proof first)

- **Asset:** GTM Evidence Workshop one-pager
- **Targets (book 5):** Google for Startups · NUS NOC · Techstars · Amazon alumni programme · Foundervine
- **Motion:** warm intro or short email → 20-min fit call → send one-pager → quote half-day
- **Do not sell:** full 8-week curriculum until a workshop is paid or LOI'd

### B. Founder-led LinkedIn (primary public)

- **Cadence:** 4–5 posts/week from the promo pack · soft CTA most days · hard CTA max 2×/week
- **Pillars:** silence after ship · evidence ladder · AI-CMO enemy · narrow buyer · pivot = win · founding seats open

### C. Communities (secondary)

Indie Hackers · r/SaaS · selective r/vibecoding · AI-builder Discords/Slacks

**Rule:** value first in OP; sprint CTA in a follow-up comment or after 2–3 value posts. No blast paste.

### D. WSS CRM + direct outreach

50/week from CRM + Apollo: Seed/pre-seed AI/SaaS, product live, no marketer, founder-led sales

**Script:** fit question first → waitlist/deposit second. The interview pipeline is the sales pipeline.

### E. Newsletter

One weekly essay adjacent to the method; CTA → waitlist / DM `SPRINT`.

### Explicitly off

Paid ads · growthforai.com (parked) · membership/Crux upsell in sprint creatives

**Public URL:** [`/growth-course`](https://wescalestartups.com/growth-course) is now the First Customers Sprint waitlist page.
