`0bfe552`. **This is the worst thing that has been found in the entity graph so far**, and it had been sitting there through six runs of entity work.

`sameAs` in schema.org means *"a URL that unambiguously indicates the item's identity"* — another page **about this same entity**. The Person node was using it as a general affiliations list:

| URL | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| `https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/` | Cambridge Judge Business School's homepage |
| `https://startup.google.com/` | Google for Startups |
| `https://www.techstars.com/` | Techstars |
| `siteConfig.linkedin` | the WSS **company** LinkedIn page |

Four of eleven entries asserted identity equivalence with organisations Daniel is not. **And it was directly undermining the fix three lines above it** — run 6 added `disambiguatingDescription` to the same node precisely because Bing surfaces seven different Daniel Johnsons for this site's queries. One half of the node was disambiguating him; the other half was merging him with Techstars.

Moved to `affiliation`, which is the correct property and preserves the signal:
- Google for Startups, Techstars → `Organization`
- Cambridge Judge Business School → `CollegeOrUniversity`

**Deliberately not `alumniOf`.** `workshops.astro` says "Guest lecturer at Cambridge Judge" and the About-page logo alt says "Visiting Lecturer". Teaching there is not studying there, and `alumniOf` would have been a false claim on a page whose whole purpose is accurate identity. Checked before writing it.

Company LinkedIn dropped from Person (it is on the Organization node already). `podcastUrl` dropped too — a page on this site is not an external identity reference.

Added `https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q137046365`. Net: **11 entries with 4 wrong → 7 that are all genuinely pages about Daniel.**

## The Wikidata finding — backlog 3 was wrong about this

**An item already exists: [Q137046365](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q137046365)**, "Daniel Cameron Johnson", *"British growth marketing consultant and founder of We Scale Startups"*, born 1994, website danieljohnson.xyz.

Created **27 Nov 2025 by the account `Danieljohnsonxyz`** — self-created. **Zero references on every statement, zero sitelinks, zero backlinks.** That is the standard profile for deletion at RfD; unsourced autobiographical items on non-notable businesspeople are actively patrolled. Not currently nominated.

**The company cannot have an item.** Wikidata notability requires one of: a Wikipedia sitelink (no), structural need from other items (no), or "a clearly identifiable entity described using **serious and publicly available references**". For a UK firm the serious reference is Companies House, and **WSS is not registered**. Self-published site and LinkedIn do not qualify.

**And the premise was shaky anyway.** Checked properly this run:
- No independent evidence that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Claude query Wikidata at inference. The 2026 Wikidata Embedding Project is opt-in MCP tooling, not something answer engines use by default.
- Google's own docs deprecate the Knowledge Graph API and point to Wikidata dumps — one input among many, no promise of a knowledge panel.
- Wikipedia is ~0.6% of AI Overview citations and <3% of Perplexity's. ChatGPT's Wikipedia reliance fell from ~55% to <20% of responses after Sept 2025.
- The "Wikidata gives 2.8× citation likelihood" class of stat has **no published methodology** and every source selling it sells Wikidata-creation services. 🔴 vendor-sourced.
- Same caution applies to schema markup generally: Ahrefs' controlled test on 1,885 pages found AI Mode +2.4%, ChatGPT +2.2%, AI Overviews −4.6%. Noise. **Keep schema because it is correct, not because it lifts citations.**

**What does correlate:** branded web mentions (Ahrefs, 75k brands, r=0.664 vs r=0.218 for backlinks). Yext's 6.8M-citation study: 86% of citations from brand-owned or brand-managed sources — 44% first-party, 42% listings/directories, 8% reviews/social.
