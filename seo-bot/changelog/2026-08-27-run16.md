## 2. The headline metric was mis-stated, and the correction matters

The task file's baseline reads "23 clicks over 28 days, all three source queries branded. Zero non-brand clicks." That is true of the **named-query** dataset and false of the site.

Two matched 28-day windows, site totals:

| | 1–28 Jul | 29 Jul–25 Aug | Δ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clicks | 36 | **68** | +89% |
| Impressions | 4,732 | 5,248 | +11% |
| CTR | 0.76% | **1.30%** | +71% |
| Position | 28.3 | 26.8 | +1.5 |

Only **three queries site-wide received any click**: `we scale startups` (16), `we scale` (3), `wescalestartups` (3). Twenty-two clicks, all brand-navigational. **The other 46 clicks — 68% of the total — came from queries Google anonymised**, and anonymisation is applied to rare, long, personally-phrased queries. Ten non-homepage URLs took 21 clicks between them and **not one of them appears anywhere in the named-query click data**:

`/about/daniel` 8 · `/resources` 4 · `/facts/we-scale-startups` 2 · `/book` 1 · `/growth-operating-system` 1 · `/industries/saas-growth` 1 · `/insights/what-is-a-growth-operating-system` 1 · `/pricing` 1 · `/services/90-day-growth-sprint` 1 · `/team` 1

Prior window the same figure was 12 non-homepage clicks and 21 anonymised. So anonymised clicks went 21 → 46 (+119%) and non-homepage clicks 12 → 21 (+75%), while named brand clicks went 15 → 22.

**Three of those clicks landed on money pages** — `/book`, `/pricing`, `/services/90-day-growth-sprint` — against two in the prior window. `/book` went 0 → 1 on 17 impressions.

This does not mean the site is winning. Three money-page clicks in 28 days is a rounding error and the primary objective is still unmet. What it means is narrower and it is worth being exact about: **"zero non-brand clicks, unmoved in fifteen runs" is a statement about the named-query table, not about the site**, and the part of the site that is growing is precisely the part GSC will not name. That is the conversational/AI-surface signature the playbook already identified, and it is the only thing in the data that is moving. Every future run should read *total clicks minus named clicks*, and the non-homepage click count, alongside the named-query table. Neither number is in the current baseline and both roughly doubled this window.

**Caveat, stated plainly:** anonymised does not prove non-brand. It proves rare. A long brand-adjacent query would be anonymised too. But brand queries are high-volume by construction and `/about/daniel` at position 3.1 taking 8 clicks with zero named brand queries behind it is not a navigational pattern. Treat this as strong evidence, not proof, and note that the honest instrument for it is conversion data, not GSC.

## A. The GSC generative-AI report is readable. Section 5 above was wrong.

Section 5 concluded the report "cannot be read through this connector" and escalated it to Daniel. The first half of that is true — `gsc_query` exposes five dimensions and `searchAppearance` returns an empty array. **The conclusion drawn from it was not.** The Search Console UI has the report, and the bot can drive the UI.

The failure mode is worth naming precisely, because it is cheap to repeat: **a connector returning nothing was treated as the data not being reachable.** One tool was tested and the search stopped there. *Do not conclude a data source is unavailable without trying the browser.*

**Access gotcha:** Chrome defaults to `admindjohnson@gmail.com`, which does not have the property — the account picker offers `daniel@wescalestartups.com`, and `/u/1/` works thereafter.

**What the report gives:** impressions only. No clicks, no CTR, no position. Pages, Countries, Devices, Days — **no QUERIES tab**, so it can never say which prompt surfaced a page. It and the AI panel are complements, not substitutes.

**Baseline: 653 AI impressions over 3 months across 50 pages; 217 over 28 days across 25.** That is 7.2/day rising to 7.75/day — about 8%, one comparison, not yet a trend.

Top pages, 28 days: `/services/90-day-growth-sprint` **37** · `/fractional-cmo-vs-agency` **18** · `/about` 16 · `/resources` 15 · `/services/fractional-cmo` 11 · `/gtm-strategy` 10 · `/services/growth-diagnosis` 9 · `/facts/we-scale-startups` **7** · `/about/daniel` 6, plus the homepage. Over 3 months the homepage takes 256 and the sprint page 151 — **62% of all AI impressions on two URLs.**

**Two findings.**

1. **The instruments corroborate.** The panel found Google AI Mode citing the sprint page and `/facts/we-scale-startups`; the report ranks them 2nd and 8th by AI impressions. Different measurement paths, same answer — the strongest evidence yet that building pages for extraction works, and the first time any GEO claim in the playbook has been confirmed twice independently.
2. **`/fractional-cmo-vs-agency` is not dead.** 574 classic impressions at position 44.2, zero clicks, two failed verdicts — and the 2nd-highest non-homepage AI-cited page at 18 impressions. Classic position is the wrong lens on it. **This does not license a third rewrite.** It means the page is consumed where rank does not apply, and should be judged on AI impressions from here.

**Also corrected: the 90-day baseline in the playbook was wrong.** It read "42 clicks / 9,500 impressions"; the UI shows **144 clicks / 16.3k impressions / 0.9% / position 25.9** for the trailing 3 months. The API's own 28-day pulls (36, then 68) reconcile with the UI and not with the old figure. Use 144 / 16.3k.

## B. GrowthMentor — the link was fine, the copy was not, and it had already leaked into an AI answer

The outreach item said "check whether the profile even links to the site". **It does** — the *We Scale Startups* experience entry links to `wescalestartups.com/`, dated August 2016, matching the unified founding year. The header website field points at `danieljohnson.xyz`, which is correct by design rather than a defect: Person roots there, Organization roots on the site. No Linktree. **Close as a non-issue.**

**Two things were wrong in our own files.** The stats recorded as "479+ sessions, 4.97/5" are, live, **4.94 / 226 reviews / 406 sessions**. Do not attribute 479 or 4.97 to GrowthMentor.

**And the actual defect.** The company blurb describes WSS as *"a growth marketing consultancy"* working with *"**early-stage** startups"* on *"paid acquisition, SEO, email"* for clients spanning *"SaaS, ecommerce, fintech and consumer apps"*. Against current positioning that is the wrong stage, a narrowed category, a channel-execution framing of the agency model the site sells against, and a client base wider than the ICP.

**The stage error has already propagated.** The one imprecision in the run-16 AI panel was Google AI Mode calling the ICP *"early-to-mid-stage SaaS founders"*. This profile is the most likely source. Section 1 above dismissed that imprecision as "not worth a page change" — correct, as it happens, because **the fix was never on a page.**

**Generalise it: when the panel reports something inaccurate, check the off-site profiles before touching a page.** The site can be right and the answer still wrong, because the engines read the profiles too.

## What this addendum changes about how runs should work

Three conclusions in this run's own log were wrong within the hour, and all three failed the same way: **a tool was consulted, it returned nothing useful, and "the tool cannot" was written down as "it cannot be known".** The playbook already carries the rule for site facts — *verify claims about the site against the site.* This extends it: **verify claims about the instruments against the instruments, and try the browser before declaring anything unreachable.**
