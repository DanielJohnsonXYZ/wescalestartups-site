## What this does and does not establish

**It does establish one thing operationally, and it is the point of the probe: a same-day re-reading carries no independent information.** Whatever the mechanism, re-running the panel within hours cannot refresh a reading. **Do not take two panel readings in one day. The minimum useful spacing is the run cadence, and this is now tested rather than assumed.**

**It does NOT establish that the panel is stable, and the distinction matters.** Two mechanisms produce an identical 5/5:

1. **The panel is genuinely stable within a session** — in which case the 2↔3 movement across the series is real between-day signal, and the series is measuring something.
2. **Google served cached AI Mode answers** for repeated queries from the same account in the same session — in which case the probe measured the cache, not the panel.

**This probe cannot distinguish them, and the second is at least as likely as the first.** The identical URL *ordering* on prompt 5 leans toward caching. **So the honest conclusion is the narrow one: same-day re-reads are worthless, and the 2↔3 question is still open.** Settling it needs two readings on the same day from *different* sessions or accounts — which is Daniel's signed-out decision (blocked item 2), not something this bot can arrange.

**What it does NOT do is license any trend claim.** Nothing here strengthens or weakens readings #1–#11.
