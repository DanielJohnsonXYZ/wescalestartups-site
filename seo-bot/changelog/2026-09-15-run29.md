**The magnitude matches what a panel should generate.** A reading cites roughly five own-domain URLs (prompt 3 ×1, prompt 5 ×3–4, prompt 6 ×1 in earlier readings). **Expected ≈5; observed ≈4.** That is a closer fit than a hypothesis of this age has any right to.

**What bounds the effect, and it is a real bound.** `/fractional-cmo-vs-agency` takes **32** Gen-AI impressions and **is never cited by any panel prompt**; `/facts/we-scale-startups`, cited by prompt 5 in every single reading, takes **27**. **A page the panel never touches outscores the panel's most-cited page.** So the bulk of the 305 is not panel-generated. A defensible upper bound is **5–6 readings in the window × ~5 URLs ≈ 25–30 of 305, i.e. 8–10% sitewide.**

**But the page-level share is where it bites.** For `/facts/we-scale-startups` the same arithmetic gives **roughly 5–6 of 27, about 20%.**

**Run 19's verdict is `/facts/we-scale-startups`, judged explicitly on AI impressions and panel prompt 5 — not rank.** Its series is **7 → 17 → 19 → 23 → 27**, and run 28 called it *"trending PASS… the best-supported positive result this bot has produced."*
