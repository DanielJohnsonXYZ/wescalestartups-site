## 6. METHOD — THREE INSTRUMENT FINDINGS

1. **PERPLEXITY HAS A COMPLETION TELL: a `<button>` whose label starts with `Finished` (`Finished · N steps`).** This closes a real hole. Run 25 read prompt 5 at **5,418 characters** on a fixed wait and again, after polling for the tell, at **8,956** — the first read was a partial render with no marker. **Every Perplexity reading before this one was taken on a fixed wait with no completion check**, which is the same failure mode that corrupted Google readings #3 and #4.
2. **THE OLD PERSONALISATION REGEX IS DEFECTIVE TWICE OVER.** `based on your` **false-positives** — it matched the innocuous *"Would you like help narrowing this down based on your company's stage…"* and would have condemned a clean prompt. And it **missed the real tell**, `Given you run`. **Correct tell: `Given you run` / `Given your work with` / any reference to WSS as the reader's own company. Never re-add `based on your` alone.**
3. **The em-dash instruction had only ever been applied to AI Mode.** Run 25 issued the hyphen variant of prompt 6 to Perplexity, got a materially different answer, discarded it and re-took the prompt with `%E2%80%94`. **It applies to every engine.**

Two smaller ones: **`document.body.innerText` on `search.google.com` is refused by the permission classifier** — read the table nodes, not the body; and **clicking a GSC tab by coordinate or by `[role="tab"]` frequently does not register**, because the tab strip is duplicated in the DOM (stale + live). **Use the URL: `&breakdown=date|page|query`.**
