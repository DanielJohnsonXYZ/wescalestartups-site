## Why nothing shipped

The obvious move is to state the positive ICP on `/book`. It fails the seven tests on **test 6**, and it nearly failed on test 1:

- **`/book` is not missing qualification copy.** It already carries `BEFORE YOU BOOK — This call isn't for everyone`, with four disqualifiers (pre-idea/pre-revenue, no sales motion, wants junior execution, not ready to act), plus honest free-vs-paid labelling and a Scorecard fallback. What it does not carry is the *positive* ICP: B2B SaaS and AI, Seed–Series B, £1m–£10m ARR. **Read the page before diagnosing it.** The first hypothesis of this run — "no qualification on the booking page" — was false.
- **The booking form accepts what the page tells people to hold off on.** The Cal.com `Stage` field offers `pre-pmf` and books it. Two of the seven chose it. That is the sharpest lever in the funnel and it is **no-touch** — the embed and the Cal.com instance are off-limits.
- **Unmeasurable at this volume.** Four website-sourced bookings per 28 days, zero qualified. A 14-day review sees ~2 bookings. A change cannot be read either way, and the baseline contains no qualified booking at all, so the natural rate is unknown.
- **Intent unknown, and the downside is real.** Daniel runs mentoring, a podcast and a referral network. Several of these calls may be deliberate, not leakage. Suppressing them unattended could destroy something he wants. This is the blocked-on-Daniel item.

Daniel has access to dozens of GA4 accounts. **Assert the property name in the header before reading a number.**
- **The key event is `book_call`** — 36 events / 20 users. It fires on `/` (15) and `/book` (12), i.e. it is a **CTA-click event, not a completed booking**. Do not read it as bookings. A separate `sticky_book_cta_shown` fires 358 times / 277 users. `ai_referral` fires 18 / 17.
