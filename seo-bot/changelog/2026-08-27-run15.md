## The finding

The 90-day sprint cluster is the site's best unclaimed non-brand demand, and nothing on the site answers it.

Four queries, 28 days (2026-07-28 to 2026-08-24), all zero clicks:

All four land on `/services/90-day-growth-sprint` — the £8k–£12k sales page (278 impressions, position 13.0, 1 click). A `page,query` diff and a directory listing confirmed the gap: 30 insights, **none** on the topic. The only other 90-day URL is the planner lead magnet at 2 impressions.

So someone searching *how to run a 90 day growth sprint* is shown a quote page. That is the entire explanation for zero clicks at position 11. The snippet answers a question they did not ask.

## Why this and not the bigger cluster

The fractional-CMO-vs-agency family is larger — 196 impressions at positions 22–28, over 300 if you widen it. It was skipped deliberately. Run 1 targeted it, the verdict closes tomorrow as a **second negative** (740 named-query impressions, weighted position 46.2, zero clicks against the 44.1 that motivated the change), and the playbook already records the remainder as authority-bound at 45+.

The sprint cluster is a different diagnosis. This is **run 9's pattern, not run 1's**: the constraint is absence of an answer, not authority. The proof is that Google already ranks WSS at 11–15 on the topic with nothing behind it — so the term is winnable on content alone, without new links. That is the distinction that should drive the pick, and it is worth stating because the two clusters look superficially similar and the bigger one is the wrong bet.

`growth operating system` (89 @ 22.3) was the other candidate and was excluded because run 14 shipped against it four hours earlier.

## What shipped

New insight, 2,623 words, single file. Content only.

- **Direct-answer definition as the opening sentence**, measured at **7% of page depth** in the rendered HTML. Current GEO research this month puts roughly 55% of AI Overview citations in the first 30% of the cited page, so the definition leads rather than concludes. Worth carrying forward as a positioning rule, not a one-off.
- **Week-by-week structure** — week 1 name the constraint and baseline the measure, weeks 2–4 design and launch 3–5 tests, month 2 scale/stop/fix and repair leaks, month 3 transfer ownership, day 90 review. Sourced from the service JSON's own `process` field and the homepage 90-day breakdown, so the guide and the sales page agree rather than compete.
- **A "why 90 days" section** answering the `why` query head-on: 30 days reads noise (paid still learning, content unindexed, outbound mid-cycle), a year lets a wrong bet become an organisational fact.
- **Four named failure modes**, including running a sprint pre-PMF.
- **8 FAQs in FAQPage schema**, written against the observed query shapes plus the retainer comparison and the cost question. For passage extraction, not rich results — the deprecation is noted and this is not a bid for them.
- **Honest labelling.** The method is given away in full; the paid engagement is named at its published £8k–£12k and framed as judgement and handover, not execution hours. The Growth Diagnosis is offered as the cheaper answer to the constraint question alone.

No new claims. Prices, duration and process match the service JSON and `/insights/fractional-cmo-cost-uk`. No invented figures, outcomes or clients.

## Carry forward

- The pick that mattered was diagnostic, not tactical: two clusters, similar shape, opposite diagnoses. Ask whether the page Google chose can *possibly* satisfy the query before assuming the constraint is authority.
