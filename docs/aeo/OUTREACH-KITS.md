## 5. Reddit answer drafts (disclose affiliation)

Use when a founder asks FCMO vs agency / cost / who to hire. Answer-first, 1–2 stats, disclose, one link max.

### r/SaaS — “Should I hire a fractional CMO or an agency?”

Hire an **agency** when the channel plan is already clear and you need production capacity.

Hire a **fractional CMO** when *you* are still the bottleneck on priorities, channels run in isolation, and nobody owns the weekly scale/stop/fix call.

Many Seed–B teams need both, in that order: diagnosis + system design first, then agency execution inside it.

Disclosure: I run a UK fractional CMO / acquisition-system practice (We Scale Startups). Public bands start at £5k/mo for ~1 day/week; short diagnosis from £2k. Happy to answer specifics in-thread either way.

### r/startups — “What does a fractional CMO cost in the UK?”

For post-PMF startups, public UK retainers I’ve seen (and publish myself) usually sit roughly **£5k–£12k/month**, driven by days/week and operating depth — not a cheaper media buyer.

Compare that to (a) agency retainers for channel production and (b) a £120–180k full-time CMO package, not to a junior marketer day rate.

I publish: FCMO from £5k/mo, Plus from £7.5k/mo, Diagnosis from £2k. Methodology write-up: https://wescalestartups.com/insights/fractional-cmo-cost-uk
