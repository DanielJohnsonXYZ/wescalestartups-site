A bare tool-name `ToolSearch` that misses is **not** evidence of absence — it cannot distinguish "absent" from "not loaded yet". **`RefreshMcpTools` names a server as `not_connected` or still connecting, and `ToolSearch` waits for connecting servers.** One of those is now mandatory before any connector is recorded as unavailable. Added as **Trap 13** and written into the access notes.
